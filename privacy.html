Privacy Policy – CleanLock

Last updated: October 29, 2026

CleanLock is developed by Murtadha Qahtan (Microsoft Store publisher “rul”).

CleanLock does not collect, record, store, transmit or share any personal data or keystrokes.

  • Offline only. The app never connects to the internet for its features. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting, accounts or third-party SDKs. (Microsoft Store handles purchase; the app itself does not phone home.)
  • Keyboard and mouse. While locked, the app blocks input with a low-level hook so keystrokes and (optionally) mouse events do not reach other apps. Input is not logged, saved or sent. Unlock combinations are detected in memory only.
  • Settings (shortcuts, language, options) are stored in a local file on your PC and are removed when you uninstall. An optional debug log of app events (never key contents) stays on your PC.
  • Children. The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft Store; the developer receives no payment details.

Contact: hibari@mail.grokbot.com

Política de privacidad (resumen)

CleanLock no recopila, graba, almacena, transmite ni comparte datos personales ni pulsaciones. Funciona sin conexión (salvo la comprobación de licencia de Microsoft Store), sin telemetría ni anuncios. Bloquea la entrada mientras está bloqueado; no la registra.

Datenschutz (Kurzfassung)

CleanLock erfasst, speichert, überträgt oder teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten und keine Tastatureingaben. Die App arbeitet offline (außer der Microsoft-Store-Lizenzprüfung), ohne Telemetrie und Werbung. Eingaben werden während der Sperre blockiert, nie aufgezeichnet.

プライバシー ポリシー (概要)

CleanLock は個人データやキー入力を収集・保存・送信・共有しません。オフラインで動作し、テレメトリや広告はありません。ロック中は入力を遮断しますが記録しません。

سياسة الخصوصية (ملخص)

لا يجمع CleanLock أي بيانات شخصية أو ضغطات مفاتيح ولا يخزّنها ولا يرسلها. يعمل دون إنترنت (عدا فحص ترخيص متجر Microsoft) ودون تتبّع أو إعلانات. يُحجب الإدخال أثناء القفل ولا يُسجَّل.
